BMW

About

BMW (German: Bayerische Motoren Werke AG) is a German multinational automotive manufacturer headquartered in Munich, Bavaria, Germany. It was founded in 1916 as a manufacturer of aircraft engines and later expanded into motorcycle and automobile production.

History

The company was founded by Karl Rapp and Gustav Otto in 1916 as a manufacturer of aircraft engines. In 1928, the company began producing motorcycles and automobiles. BMW became known for its high-performance vehicles and luxury cars.
