BUGATTI

About

Bugatti is a French luxury performance-car marque known for extraordinary speed, craftsmanship, and distinctive design. Its modern cars combine handcrafted detail with powerful engines and advanced aerodynamics.

History

Ettore Bugatti founded the company in Molsheim in 1909. Bugatti became famous for elegant racing and road cars, including the Type 35. In the modern era, the Veyron and Chiron continued the brand's tradition of engineering at the limits of performance.
