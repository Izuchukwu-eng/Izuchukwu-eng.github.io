FERRARI

About

Ferrari is an Italian manufacturer of high-performance sports cars, grand tourers, and racing cars. Its cars are known for expressive styling, powerful engines, and a strong connection to motorsport.

History

Enzo Ferrari founded Scuderia Ferrari in 1929 to support racing drivers. The company began building road cars in 1947, and the prancing horse soon became one of the world's most recognizable symbols of performance and Italian craftsmanship.
