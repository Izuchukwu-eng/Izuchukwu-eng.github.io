FORD

About

Ford Motor Company is an American automobile manufacturer producing cars, trucks, SUVs, and electric vehicles. Ford is known for practical engineering, durable vehicles, and models that have shaped everyday motoring and performance driving.

History

Henry Ford founded the company in 1903. Ford transformed automobile manufacturing through moving assembly-line production, helping make cars more accessible. The Model T, Mustang, F-Series, and Bronco became landmark vehicles in the company's history.
