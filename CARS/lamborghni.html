LAMBORGHINI

About

Automobili Lamborghini is an Italian manufacturer of luxury sports cars and SUVs. The brand is celebrated for dramatic styling, powerful engines, and an unmistakable connection to high-performance driving.

History

Ferruccio Lamborghini founded the company in Sant'Agata Bolognese in 1963. Lamborghini quickly became known for innovative grand touring cars, and models such as the Miura, Countach, and Aventador helped define the modern supercar.
