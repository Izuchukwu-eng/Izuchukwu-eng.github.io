MERCEDES-BENZ

About

Mercedes-Benz is a German luxury vehicle manufacturer known for combining elegant design, advanced engineering, and comfort. The company produces passenger cars, performance vehicles, electric models, and commercial vehicles.

History

Mercedes-Benz traces its origins to Karl Benz's Patent-Motorwagen, widely recognized as the world's first practical automobile, built in 1886. The Mercedes-Benz brand was formed in 1926 when Benz & Cie. and Daimler-Motoren-Gesellschaft merged.
